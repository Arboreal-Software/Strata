## Drain receipt and trust contract

A terminal receipt is not a boolean “drained” setter. Its private construction must bind the allocation incarnation, terminal admission generation and complete declared consumer device set; the actual checking function produces it only after successful drain and restoration. Keep runtime/session/lease owners alive throughout. A trusted internal caller must establish host/IO producer closure before checking devices; the type cannot prove arbitrary application thread behavior. No future use/submission or concurrent callback reentry is allowed after seal. Raw pointers cannot be revoked after escape, so selected-borrow and owner-reactor serialization remain explicit obligations.

Reuse the driver's already checked barrier only after matching this coverage/ordering, including zero-handle partial construction. A receipt cannot clear poison or make an allocation publishable; health stays terminal across aliases. All pool/range preparation succeeds before the first free. Drain/selection/restoration failure before commit retains the complete gang and charges. A failure during destructive commit cannot undo earlier successful frees: preserve exact residual ownership and no-retry state, or fail-stop; never claim rollback/all-original-bytes retained after successful partial destruction.
