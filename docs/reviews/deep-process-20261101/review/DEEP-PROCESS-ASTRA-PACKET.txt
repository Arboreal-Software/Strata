- **Integration feedback arrived too late.** Too much work accumulated before exercising the real startup → admission → prefix replay → refusal or repair → continued generation sequence.
- **Test construction became a substantial second project.** Extracted-source adapters, recording objects, frozen snapshots, readers, and packaging each introduced their own failure modes. Some are necessary, but their cost wasn’t being weighed against the uncertainty they removed.
